A 97-word villain monologue from the usurper queen of Saltkeep, wearing her
brother Edric's crown. She has held the keep for three years on salt pork,
rainwater, and a council that eats the stores — and she does not threaten the
hero at the gate. She hands the waiting over: the crown still pinches, the
gate stays open, and the waiting is yours now.

The program writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt`. Node 22, no
dependencies, no network access.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```
